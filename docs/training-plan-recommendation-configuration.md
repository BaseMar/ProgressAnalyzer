# Training plan configuration for the recommendation system

Status: the proposal was approved and saved on 8 November 2026. Version 6
(ID 15) takes effect on 10 November 2026. The tables compare it with source
version ID 14, effective from 9 November. Earlier versions and results are retained.

This is the configuration record for that release. For current starting-load
behavior, see the [progression rules](progression-redesign-rules.md); the
9 November update supersedes the manual-start policy described below.

The user requested revised repetition ranges and RIR targets. Exercise order and
set counts stay the same; bold entries identify changed settings. These are
practical settings for hypertrophy and strength, rather than scientifically
established individual optima.

Main presses and the squat use 5-8 reps to combine strength work with repetition
progression. The Romanian deadlift uses 6-10 reps and RIR 3. Pulling movements
and other presses mostly use 6-12 reps at RIR 2. Most isolation exercises use
10-20 reps at RIR 1 or 2; overhead extensions and French presses use 10-15 at RIR 2.

The latest dead hang entry was 35 s, supporting a proposed 20-45 s range instead
of 60-80 s. Plank had no recent timed baseline: the proposed range was 30-60 s
instead of 60-70 s, with the starting duration established during execution.
Neither timed exercise uses RIR.

The user confirmed a 5 kg increment for Overhead Triceps Extension. Other
increments are retained from the plan. The 10% increase limit may require a
manual decision for light dumbbells or large weight-stack increments.

Calendar: retain the 2026-11-01 start and six-week cycle, with an automatic deload
in the final week. Deload retains sets and repetitions, reduces loads by 30-40%
and sets target RIR to 3-4.

At the time of approval, users chose unestablished starting loads manually with
history visible. Weight increments use the same convention as recorded loads,
such as per dumbbell. Lack of a smaller increment does not justify inventing
unavailable microloading.

## Evidence and verification

[ACSM 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/) and
[Currier et al. 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37414459/) support considering
strength and hypertrophy goals when choosing loads and volume. They do not
establish one exact repetition range or RIR for each exercise listed here.

Saving a configuration change creates a new version. Historical plans and results
remain intact. Under the release's original policy, a substantial range or RIR
change required a manual starting load, following user decision 6B. Current
starting-load behavior is documented in the linked progression rules.
